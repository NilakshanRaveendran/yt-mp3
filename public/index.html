YouTube → MP3

Paste a YouTube video link and download the audio as MP3.

Fast mode prioritizes shorter conversion time and smaller files, with less audio detail than Highest quality.

MP3 conversion re-encodes the entire audio file, so long recordings can take several minutes. Fast mode uses 128 kbps and a faster encoder setting. Highest quality uses --audio-quality 0 and can take longer. The progress bar tracks both downloading and conversion, including audio processed and estimated time remaining.
